# UICS — Unified Intelligent Control Stack  
### Original Framework by Neeraj Aggarwal

## 1. Purpose
UICS defines the enterprise‑wide control plane for all payment flows.  
It unifies fraud, AML, sanctions, pricing, entitlements, and delegated authority into a single intelligent layer.

## 2. Components

### 2.1 Policy Engine
Evaluates:
- Customer rules  
- Product rules  
- Regulatory constraints  
- Delegated authority  

### 2.2 Intelligence Layer
- Fraud scoring  
- AML detection  
- Behavioral analytics  
- AI‑driven recommendations  

### 2.3 Pricing & Entitlements
- Fee calculation  
- Tiering  
- Limits  
- Customer‑specific rules  

### 2.4 Evidence & Audit Fabric
- Immutable logs  
- Explainability  
- Decision traceability  
- Regulatory reporting  

## 3. Integration Points
- Multi‑rail orchestration  
- ISO 20022 validation  
- Exception workflows  
- Tokenized money rails  

## 4. Benefits
- Consistency  
- Compliance  
- Transparency  
- AI‑native augmentation
