## 1. Overview
Tokenized deposits represent **digitally native liabilities of regulated banks**, issued on distributed or hybrid ledgers while maintaining full parity with traditional deposit accounts.  
They enable programmable settlement, atomic transfers, and real‑time liquidity visibility across enterprise payment systems.

## 2. Core Characteristics
- **1:1 redeemability** with traditional deposits  
- **Issued by regulated banks** (not synthetic assets)  
- **Ledger‑agnostic representation** (DLT, permissioned chains, hybrid rails)  
- **Programmable settlement** via smart‑contract logic  
- **Interoperable** with ACH, RTP, wires, and cross‑border corridors  

## 3. Architecture Components

### 3.1 Tokenization Engine

## 4. Key Use Cases
